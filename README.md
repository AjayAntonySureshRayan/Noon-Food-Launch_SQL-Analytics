# Noon Food Launch SQL Analytics
On 1st May 2024, Noon — one of the largest e-commerce companies in the Middle East — launched a new Food Delivery vertical in Dubai.
As part of the Data Analytics team, I was tasked to extract, analyze, and interpret key performance metrics from the company’s order data to evaluate the launch success and provide insights for the Growth and Product teams.
